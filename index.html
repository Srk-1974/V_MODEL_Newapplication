Welcome to the AutoVModel AI Advisor — your automotive systems engineering co-pilot.

I specialize in V Model methodology for automotive projects. To get started, tell me about your project:

  • Vehicle system: powertrain, safety, infotainment, chassis, ADAS?
  • Project scope: new ECU development, software update, system integration?

  • Safety level: ASIL A–D or QM?
